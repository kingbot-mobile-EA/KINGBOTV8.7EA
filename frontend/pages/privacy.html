Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how GIBSONFX TECH ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the KingBot V8.7 MICRO-FLIP platform (the "Service"). By creating an account, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — used for verification, login, and notifications
  • Phone number — used for SMS OTP verification and trade alerts
  • Password — stored as a bcrypt hash, never in plaintext
  • Display name — optional, for personalization

Trading Information

  • Broker server and MT5 login — to connect your EA account
  • EA status data — balance, equity, trades, settings synced from your EA bridge
  • License keys — generated per subscription, used for EA authentication

Usage Data

  • Activity logs (logins, settings changes, subscription events)
  • Theme and notification preferences

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account and secure access to the platform
  • To connect and monitor your EA accounts via the mobile bridge
  • To process and manage your subscription and license keys
  • To provide analytics, trade history, and performance reports
  • To send notifications and alerts based on your preferences
  • To provide AI support based on the KingBot V8.7 trading guide
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties
  • We do not share your trading data with other users
  • We do not access your broker account funds or execute withdrawals
  • We do not store your MT5 password — the EA bridge uses license keys, not credentials
  • We do not use your data for advertising

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Render for the backend, static hosting for the frontend). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. API communication is encrypted with TLS. Database access is restricted to the application layer.

5. AI Support

The AI support chat uses a rule-based system trained on the KingBot V8.7 trading guide. If the OpenAI-compatible fallback is enabled, your chat messages may be sent to an external AI provider. No personal account data is included in AI queries — only the text you type in the chat.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your data in Profile settings
  • Correction: You can update your display name, theme, and notifications
  • Deletion: You can cancel your subscription and request account deletion
  • Export: Your trading data is visible in Analytics and can be reviewed

7. Cookies & Local Storage

The platform uses local storage (not cookies) to store your JWT session token, theme preference, and user profile. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to third parties. Clearing your browser data removes this information.

8. Third-Party Services

The platform integrates with: email delivery (SMTP) for verification, SMS gateway (Twilio) for OTP, payment processor (Stripe) for subscriptions, and optionally an AI provider for chat support. Each operates under their own privacy policy. We share only the minimum data necessary for each service to function.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data while your subscription is active and for 90 days after cancellation, after which trading data is anonymized. Account credentials and license keys are retained for security and audit purposes.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes via email. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact GIBSONFX TECH through the AI support widget or your account profile.
